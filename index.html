My design process

1

Understand

Who is it for, and what should they do after seeing it?
2

Research

Look at real examples, real data and what already works.
3

Sketch

Plan layout, colour palette and fonts on paper first.
4

Design

Build it in code or Canva with a clear focal point.
5

Test

Check on phone and laptop; is the text easy to read?
6

Refine

Take feedback, simplify, and polish the details.

Principles I design by

🎯 One clear message👁 Strong hierarchy🎨 Consistent colour & type🔍 High contrast, easy to read🌬 Generous white space✨ Motion with a purpose
